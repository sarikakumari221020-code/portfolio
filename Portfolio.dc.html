(01) — About

A little about how I think.

I'm an MDes student at UPES building my practice around human-centered design. I'm most interested in the space where people and interfaces meet — understanding users, finding patterns in messy behaviour, and questioning the assumptions everyone else treats as settled.

My work moves from observation to insight to interface — then back to testing, because a design isn't finished until real people have used it. I care about the small details that make a product feel like it quietly understands you.

How the work moves
Observe → Understand → Explore → Prototype → Test

(03) — Method

How I solve problems.

Hover a stage to see how it works — on mobile, tap.

01

Research

"Listen before designing."

I study people in their real context — interviews, observation, secondary data — before a single screen exists.

02

Define

"Turn observations into a clear problem."

I cluster what I hear into patterns and reframe them into a single problem that's actually worth solving.

03

Ideate

"Explore multiple possibilities."

I generate several honest directions and pressure-test each one against the evidence, not against my taste.

04

Prototype

"Make ideas tangible."

I build interactive flows fast, so an idea can be felt and reacted to — not just described in a deck.

05

Test

"Learn from real behaviour."

I put prototypes in front of real users, watch what they actually do, and iterate on the gaps.

Beyond the interface.
